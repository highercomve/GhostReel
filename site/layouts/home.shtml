Your computer does the work

Index the footage.
Keep the footage.

In standalone mode, speech recognition, frame descriptions and search embeddings run in helper processes inside GhostReel, on your GPU. Nothing leaves your machine, and your videos are only read: never modified or copied.

  1. 01
    Download the modelsOpen the Models page and pick what you need, once.
  2. 02
    Add a folder and index itTranscripts, keyframes and vectors go into one local database.
  3. 03
    Search, script and exportJump to the moment, draft the cut, hand it to your editor.

You can also point a capability at a model server or a coding-agent CLI you already use. Then frames, transcripts and prompts go to that server or tool. Keep each capability on This computer to keep the work on your machine.

For terminals and AI agents

Script it. Or let an agent edit.

The ghostreel command line covers indexing, search, transcripts, scripts, exports and models. ghostreel mcp speaks the Model Context Protocol, so an AI agent can index footage, search it and build an edit.

  1. 01
    Learn the footagecreate_project, add_folder, index, then search, get_transcript, get_video.
  2. 02
    Build the editdraft_script, or save_script with your own JSON. Clips outside the footage come back as issues.
  3. 03
    Hand it offexport_script writes FCP 7 XML or .otio.
